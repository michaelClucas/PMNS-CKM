## Criterion-4 protocol (adopted iteration 3, from exp 012)
1. Freeze pipeline, selection score, hit definition before running fakes. Each fake reruns everything, including discrete and ordering choices.
2. Mandatory nulls, per ordering: N1 CKM-side hierarchical (real PMNS + tables, fake CKM with λ 0.05-0.5, A 0.3-2, ρ̄, η̄ ∈ [-1,1]); N2 shifted data (real CKM, PMNS centre from a smooth prior not built from pattern values, scored with translated NuFIT tables). Haar-PMNS optional.
3. Hit: a fake's best relation has k ≤ N-2 under the same counting, max|pull| < 2, p_global ≥ the real candidate's.
4. 1000 fakes per null and ordering; pass if the one-sided 95% Clopper-Pearson upper limit < 1% (≤ 4 hits). Early stop only to declare failure.
5. Power check first: 20 injections of the candidate's own structure (best-fit prediction + measurement noise) through the full pipeline with 300 fakes each. If < 50% pass, the null cannot confirm or exclude this family.
6. Leak probe, alias check, ordering bit, k_d in bits run alongside.
7. Wording: a failed null = "no evidence". "Excluded" only for fit-quality failure or a null with demonstrated power.

## Open questions
- MSSM needs 2-loop Yukawa RGEs for < 2% accuracy on θ23^q, θ13^q.
- Can any family reach k ≤ N-2? Every family tried (F1, F2, symbolic, SO(10) MN/FN, BSV) either has free phases/θν/Yukawa entries that absorb the CKM, or fails fit quality. Need families where quark and lepton sectors share a small number of continuous parameters by construction. Best-known class in the literature: modular flavour symmetry with a common modulus τ for quarks and leptons (few couplings, many observables).

## Assessment after 15 iterations
Seven families searched, all negative: shared rotation (F1), Clebsch corrections (F2), symbolic formulas, minimal and real SO(10), BSV type-II, modular flavour (literature + common-τ catalog). (List from iteration 5; later families in the entries above.) The recurring pattern: lepton mixing is always fitted by sector-specific freedom (phases, θν, Yukawa entries, couplings); whenever the quark and lepton sectors are forced to share parameters (BSV, common τ), the quark sector fails at PDG precision. The CKM is never used to predict PMNS. Exp 020 ran the reverse direction (leptons fix τ, predict quarks) and it fails too; exp 021 bounds CKM-specific PMNS information at ≲ 1-2 bits in the families tried. Exps 022-023: the two most compressive published GUT flavour models found by the scout fail as well; neither actually feeds the CKM into PMNS (both get PMNS from a lepton-only seesaw structure), and the scout's description of P1 was wrong, so literature-derived model descriptions must be checked against the paper before a test is designed. Iteration 8: the verified scout finds the published CKM → PMNS maps are almost all inside F1/F2; the cross-family NN test finds ~1.5 bits of CKM information at the ~2σ level, attributable to the size of λ rather than CKM detail, and fragile to the prior mixture and seed. Iteration 9: the decisive pre-registered version (exp 028) removes even that: with converged-er flows and a λ-matched null the real CKM is at the median of fakes (rank 0.52), and no family reweighting changes this. Within everything implemented (11 model families, generic shared textures, all published CKM → PMNS maps verified in exp 027), there is no evidence that the CKM carries PMNS information beyond the Cabibbo scale. What remains testable without new physics input: the frozen simple-relation list against future JUNO/DUNE/Hyper-K data (exp 029). Iteration 10: the skeptic audit (exp 031) changes no candidate verdict but shows the "≲ 1-2 bit" figure is an instrument ceiling, and the power test (exp 032) shows that information beyond (λ, A) is undetectable at today's precision below ≈ 7-8 bits by any estimator. The program's negative results are therefore "no evidence", and the open question that is testable now is whether the CKM's Cabibbo-scale structure (λ, A) carries PMNS information, which needs the plain N1 null plus a power test against λ-type links. Iteration 11 answers that: no estimator can detect a Cabibbo-scale link below ≈ 7 bits at today's precision (≈ 4-5 bits at JUNO/DUNE/Hyper-K precision), and the NN pipeline built in exps 025/028 has no power at all against such links. The reverse direction hits the same ≈ 8-bit ceiling. The untested classes from the skeptic's list (invariant relations, Dirac Y_ν = Y_u, Δm² ratio) give no evidence. Conclusion so far: with present data the search is precision-limited, not search-limited. The only test with real discriminating power in the near term is the frozen simple-relation list (exp 029) against JUNO's sin²θ12 and DUNE/Hyper-K's δ_CP and θ23. Iteration 12 (user chose: targeted tests + continued exploration): the likelihood statistic (exp 037) does reach usable power where the NN had none, but today no frozen relation shows evidence, and what the real CKM adds is λ only. Two new compressive shared-parameter classes (yukawaon, universal four-zero/democracy) both fail fit quality by > 10σ, again because sharing forces quark-like structure on leptons (or vice versa for mass ratios). Same pattern as BSV and common τ. Iteration 13: a verified literature scout (26 papers) and a compressive-by-construction texture grammar add six more shared-parameter structures that pass compression and fail fit quality by ≥ 10σ (BPR, Albright-Barr, Δ(27) UTZ, grammar subset). Every compressive quark-lepton model tested so far (11 of them) fails fit quality at PDG + NuFIT 6.1 precision; models that fit all have k ≥ N. Iteration 14: the last two open published models fail too (AKS χ² 161, CM χ² 764), and the diagnostic (exp 043) shows the failure is counting, not a specific shared property: in random shared textures the pass rate is 0/233 at k_cont ≤ N-3 and rises steadily with k_cont - N. Quark textures used so far spend ≥ 9 parameters on 8 quark observables, so nothing is left to predict leptons. The remaining route to compression is a quark sector that is itself predictive (≤ 6 parameters for 8 observables). Iteration 15: such quark sectors exist (Harrison-Scott with k 6; 19 grammar textures with k 6, though their selection costs ≈ 16 bits), but the simplest lepton extensions of the grammar textures (Georgi-Jarlskog + Y_ν = Y_u) fail by > 50σ on m_μ/m_τ, and the literature survivors mostly have no lepton extension at all. Light-quark mass precision (PDG 2026, ~2%) is the binding constraint that published "good fits" evade with 12-53% errors.
